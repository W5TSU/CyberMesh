Range probe

Asks the tracked node for its position over the private channel on a timer. A reply is a confirmed round trip home and comes back at full GPS precision; a timeout is recorded as a miss — the one thing passive position logging can never tell you. A reply that carries no fix is tracked separately: the radio path worked, so it counts as reaching home, it just can't be placed on the map. Probing only runs while the node is moving, detected from its own beacons, so a night parked at home costs nothing.
